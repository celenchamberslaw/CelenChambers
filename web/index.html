Who We Are

Two Decades of Legal Excellence

Celen LawFirm is a full-service law firm based in Ankara, Turkey. We are a premier practice with a national and international client base. Our expertise includes but is not limited to corporate and commercial law, employment and labour law, pharmaceutical and healthcare law, consumer law, litigation, competition law, international law, and tax law.

Founded on the principles of integrity, diligence, and strategic thinking, we have built lasting relationships with clients ranging from multinational corporations to individuals seeking trusted legal counsel. Our approach combines traditional legal craftsmanship with modern efficiency.
